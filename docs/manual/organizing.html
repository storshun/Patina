Folders, solo and smart materials

A real texture quickly grows to twenty or thirty layers. Folders keep them tidy and let you mask a whole group at once. Solo shows what one layer actually contributes. Smart materials save a finished stack, so you can use it again on another model. And the History panel lets you step back through every change, sliders included.

Folders

A folder groups layers. Everything inside it is composited first. The result then goes on top of the layers below the folder, through the folder's own opacity, blend mode, channels, mask and smart mask. Folders can hold other folders.

To…Do this
Make a folder Folder. It goes above the selected layer, or inside the selected folder.
Put a layer in a new folderSelect it and press Ctrl+G (Group into a folder).
Move a layer into a folderDrag it onto the middle of the folder's row. Or right-click it ▸ Move into folder.
ReorderDrag a layer onto the top or bottom half of another row (an orange line shows where it goes). move it within its folder.
Take a layer outDrag it out of the folder, or right-click ▸ Move out of the folder.
Open or close a folderClick the arrow on its row. The number after the name counts the layers directly inside it.

New layers go above the selection. If a folder is selected, they go to the top of that folder. Deleting or duplicating a folder deletes or duplicates everything in it.

Masking a whole groupPut a base fill, an edge-wear fill and a dirt fill in one folder and give the folder a black mask. Painting the folder's mask now reveals the whole worn-metal look at once, instead of painting three masks. An adjustment layer inside a folder only acts where the folder's mask lets it.

Solo: see what one layer does

Alt+click a layer's eye (visibility box) to solo it. Only that layer (or folder) is shown, over the base material. The eye gets a yellow ring and a Solo: … button appears above the list. Click that button, or Alt+click the eye again, to show everything. Alt+S solos the selected layer.

Solo is only a way of looking. It does not change any layer's visibility, it is not saved, and exports always contain the whole stack. To export one layer on its own, use Export ▸ Only the selected layer (see Export).

Color tags, names and layer settings

  • Color tags: pick a color under the layer's name in the properties, or right-click ▸ Color tag. A strip of that color marks the row.
  • Rename: double-click the name in the list, or edit the Name field.
  • Copy and paste settings: right-click ▸ Copy layer settings, then Paste layer settings on another layer. This copies the opacity, blend mode, channels, channel opacities and smart mask. Between two fill layers it also copies the material, and between two adjustment layers the adjustment.
  • Fill with a black mask: the arrow next to Fill layer ▸ Fill layer with a black mask adds a fill that stays hidden until you paint its mask. This is the usual way to paint a material onto part of a model.
  • Clear paint: Clear paint in a paint layer's properties (or the right-click menu) removes everything painted on the layer and keeps its mask. It is undoable.

Smart materials

If smart materials are new to you, the beginner's guide builds one from scratch and explains what carries over to other models.

A smart material is a saved layer or folder: usually a few fill layers with smart masks, plus adjustments. Because smart masks are worked out from the shape of the model, the same smart material looks right on any mesh. The Smart materials tab of the Materials panel shows them all: click one to add it above the selected layer, or drag it to where you want it in the stack (or onto the model). Smart materials above the layer list has them too.

A smart material arrives as one folder. Open it and change anything: drag a material from the Materials tab onto one of its fill layers to swap it (the smart mask stays), drag a smart mask onto a layer, copy a mask from one layer to another by dragging its thumbnail, move a smart mask's Level, or paint the folder's mask (Drag and drop lists them all). Patina bakes the AO and curvature maps the smart masks need if they are not baked yet.

Saving your own

Right-click a layer or folder ▸ Save as smart material… (or Smart materials ▸ Save the selected layer…). Give it a name, then decide whether to include painted textures and masks.

  • Without paint (the default), the smart material keeps only settings: layers, materials, smart masks, adjustments, opacities and blend modes. It works on any mesh.
  • With paint, painted channels and masks are stored too. Use this to copy hand-painted layers between projects of the same model. Paint follows the UV layout, so on a different mesh it lands in the wrong places.

Your smart materials are JSON files in ~/.local/share/patina/user/smart_materials (on Windows, %LOCALAPPDATA%\Patina\user\smart_materials). Smart materials ▸ Open the smart materials folder takes you there. To share one, copy its file to another machine. A file with the same name as a built-in one replaces it.

Undo and the History panel

Everything that changes the project can be undone: strokes, fills, layers added, deleted, moved or grouped, every layer setting (opacity, blend mode, channels, materials, smart masks, adjustments, names, color tags), masks added or removed, Clear paint, smart materials and UV edits. Dragging a slider makes one step, recorded when you let go.

The History panel (next to Properties) lists the steps. Click one to go back to that point. Later steps stay grayed out until you change something, and then they are dropped, as with undo.

Patina keeps the last 100 steps, or 1 GB of undo data on the graphics card, whichever comes first. Painting stores only the parts of the textures that changed. Opening a project, changing the texture resolution or loading a new mesh starts a new history.

Gotchas

Painting on a folder paints its maskAs with fill and adjustment layers, the brush edits the folder's mask. A folder without a mask gets a black one at the first dab, which hides everything in the folder except where you paint. If you only want to hide a few spots, use Add white mask first and then erase.
“My layer disappeared”Check for a Solo: … button above the list. Solo is still on, and it lasts until you turn it off (it is not saved with the project).
Smart materials with paint on another modelPaint is stored by UV position, not by surface position. On a different mesh it appears in random-looking places. Save without paint to share a look between models.
Undo and slider dragsA value is recorded when the slider is released. If you undo while still holding a slider, the drag is finished first and then undone as one step.
