    Patina Web

    A lightweight browser version of the Patina texture painter. Paint library materials straight onto a model: brush dabs are projected in 3D, so strokes ignore how the UVs are laid out.

    Mirror X / Y / Z in the brush settings paints both sides of a symmetric model at once.

    Layers: paint layers, fill layers (a material over the whole model; painting paints its mask) and adjustment layers (hue, saturation, brightness, contrast, roughness, metallic). Fill and adjustment layers can use a smart mask (edges, cavities, upward faces, a gradient or noise), multiplied with the painted one. Each layer has an opacity and a blend mode.

    Island brush (L): paint over a part of the model and press Enter. It becomes one UV island, unwrapped flat, turned upright, at the texel density of the rest of the layout, in free space, and pinned so later edits leave it alone. With Mirror X on, the other half is selected too and shares the island's texels. Paint moves with the UVs. In the UV view, right-click an island to relax, orient, scale or pack. Unique UVs rebuilds the UVs when a model has none, or when islands overlap.

    Save project keeps the model, layers, islands and your image materials in one .patinaweb file. + Image in the library turns a photo or texture into a material. Export the maps for glTF / Blender, Unreal or Unity, or a textured GLB.

    The desktop app adds full UV editing (seams, unwrap methods, UDIM), clone and smudge, baked AO, the material editor, the Blender bridge and Claude (MCP) automation. Everything here stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
